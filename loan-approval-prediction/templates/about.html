PROJECT NOTES

About FinSight

Loan approval prediction and EMI risk analysis using machine learning.

Dataset and scope

The model uses the bundled 4,269-row Loan Approval Prediction dataset. Its recorded predictors are number of dependents, education, self-employment, annual income, loan amount, loan term in years, CIBIL score and four asset values. Its target is the recorded loan_status.

The source dataset does not contain age, gender, marital status, occupation, current EMI, other monthly income, credit-history labels, or monthly repayment history. Those fields are not presented as features learned from this dataset. Income from the live form is annualized to match the dataset.

Dataset source on GitHub · This dataset is used as a public educational dataset; verify upstream licensing and provenance before redistribution outside coursework.

Model training

Training cleans column whitespace, removes duplicate rows, coerces numeric values, imputes missing values, one-hot encodes categoricals, standardizes numerical columns, and uses a stratified 80/20 holdout split. Random Forest is primary; Logistic Regression is evaluated as a comparison. See model/metrics.json for the measured held-out metrics from your training run.

Separate financial rules

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula. Total EMI equals existing plus proposed EMI; remaining income equals monthly household income minus total EMI. EMI-to-income and debt-to-income ratios use total EMI divided by total monthly household income. For explanation only, the app flags score under 650, poor credit history, negative remaining income, EMI ratio above 50%, or requested amount above 60 months of income. These are transparent educational heuristics, not bank policy, and do not override the model output.

Limitations

Dataset labels reflect prior outcomes and are not a universal measure of creditworthiness. Confidence is the classifier's predicted class probability, not a calibrated guarantee. The system is an educational demonstration and must not be used for real lending decisions.
